## Notes

- Lesson files are in `src/content/lessons/`, named `computer-science-1-NN-<slug>.mdx`.
- `solCodes` in frontmatter holds standard-level codes (`1.AP.1`). The lettered sub-items each lesson covers are listed at the top of the lesson body.
- Lessons run 15 to 20 minutes. `durationMinutes` equals the sum of the four timed sections.
- Lessons that need preparation open with a `## Prep` section.
- Each lesson carries one of three tags: `unplugged`, `device`, or `device-optional`.
