**Standards covered (2024 Virginia Computer Science SOL)**

- **8.IC.3** The student will identify opportunities for education, training, and preparation to enter into a chosen computer science career field.
  - a. Identify an education and training plan for a chosen computer science career.
  - b. Outline the use of computer science skills required in a chosen career.
  - c. Develop short-and long-term goals for a chosen career.
  - d. Research emerging trends in a chosen career path.

## Prep

- Ask the student to choose a computing career in advance.
- Bookmark a reliable career information source and, if possible, the course catalog of a local high school or the state's graduation and career pathway information.
- Make a template: Career, Skills required, Education and training path, Short-term goals, Long-term goals, Emerging trends.

## Warm-up (2 minutes)

Ask, "If you wanted this job in ten years, what would you need to have done by next year?"

## Direct Instruction (4 minutes)

- Explain education and training options: high school courses, career and technical programs, industry certifications, two-year and four-year degrees, apprenticeships, and self-directed learning with a portfolio.
- Explain outlining skills: the specific computer science skills used in the career, such as programming, data analysis, networking, or security, and how they are used.
- Teach goal-setting: short-term goals for this year, long-term goals for five to ten years, each specific and measurable.
- Define emerging trends: changes now under way in the field that will affect what skills are needed.

## Guided Practice (10 minutes)

1. The student researches the career's required skills and fills in the template.
2. Research the education and training paths and choose one.
3. Write three short-term and two long-term goals.
4. Research one or two emerging trends and note how they affect the plan.

## Independent Practice / Quick Check (4 minutes)

The student presents the completed plan and explains how the short-term goals lead to the long-term ones.

Look for:
- The education and training plan is realistic and sourced.
- Skills are specific to the career.
- Goals are specific and connected.

## Differentiation

**For students needing support**
- Complete the template for skills and one path.
- Write one goal of each kind.

**For students ready for more**
- Compare two paths to the same career on time and cost.
- Contact a professional or program for information.

## Extensions

- Take one action on a short-term goal this week.
- Review the plan when choosing high school courses.

## Notes for Parent/Instructor

- This lesson has four parts. Allow a second session if needed.
- Plans will change. Planning and updating is the skill.
