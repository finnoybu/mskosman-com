**Standards covered (2024 Virginia Computer Science SOL)**

- **7.CSY.1** The student will design projects that use computing devices to collect and exchange data.
  - a. Apply project management skills to distribute tasks and maintain project timeline.
  - b. Generate ideas combining hardware and software components that can be used to collect and exchange data.
  - c. Describe how hardware and software can be used together to collect and exchange data.

## Prep

- Think of a few project themes that could suit your home, such as a weather log, a plant moisture monitor, a step-count challenge, or a pet feeding record.

## Warm-up (3 minutes)

Ask, "What would you like to know about your home, your habits, or your neighborhood that a device could measure for you?"

## Direct Instruction (5 minutes)

- Define the kind of project: a system that collects data with hardware, processes it with software, and exchanges it by showing, storing, or sending it.
- Explain how hardware and software work together: a sensor or input device captures data, software records and processes it, and a network or storage passes it on.
- Generate ideas: pair a hardware option, such as a phone's sensors, a camera, a thermometer, or a microcontroller, with a software option, such as a spreadsheet, a form, or a program.
- Teach project management basics: list the tasks, assign each to a person, estimate time, order them, set milestones, and track progress on a board with to do, doing, and done columns.

## Guided Practice (8 minutes)

1. The student brainstorms at least five project ideas and picks one.
2. Describe which hardware and software it would use and how data moves between them.
3. Break the project into tasks on sticky notes and place them on the board.
4. Draw a timeline with dates and assign tasks, including any to helpers.

## Independent Practice / Quick Check (4 minutes)

The student presents the project plan: the idea, how hardware and software will collect and exchange data, the task list with owners, and the timeline.

Look for:
- The idea combines hardware and software to collect and exchange data.
- The data path is described.
- Tasks are distributed and scheduled.

## Differentiation

**For students needing support**
- Choose from the prepared themes.
- Plan five tasks over one week.

**For students ready for more**
- Add risks to the plan and how to handle them.
- Plan how data will be shared with someone else.

## Extensions

- Carry out the project over the following weeks, updating the board.
- Look at how a real team tracks tasks.

## Notes for Parent/Instructor

- The project does not need special equipment. A phone, a thermometer, and a spreadsheet are enough.
- Keep the plan. Lesson 10 selects the components.
