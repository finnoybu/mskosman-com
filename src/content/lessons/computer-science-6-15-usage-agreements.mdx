**Standards covered (2024 Virginia Computer Science SOL)**

- **6.CYB.2** The student will investigate various usage agreements designed to protect individuals.
  - a. Identify laws governing privacy with computing devices and emerging technologies.
  - b. Investigate and describe common components of usage agreements.
  - c. Identify user and company protections in a usage agreement.

## Prep

- Find a current, child-appropriate summary of one or two privacy laws that apply where you live, and check the details.
- Print or open one usage agreement or its plain-language summary.

## Warm-up (3 minutes)

Ask, "Have you ever clicked 'I agree' without reading? What do you think you agreed to?"

## Direct Instruction (5 minutes)

- Introduce the privacy laws you prepared: who each protects and what it requires.
- Define usage agreement: the rules a user accepts to use a service. Related documents include terms of service, acceptable use policies, and privacy policies.
- List common components: who may use the service and age limits, what users may and may not do, what data is collected and how it is used and shared, who owns content users post, how accounts can be closed, and how disputes are handled.
- Explain two-way protection: some clauses protect users, such as privacy commitments. Others protect the company, such as limits on its responsibility.

## Guided Practice (7 minutes)

1. Skim the agreement together and find four of the components.
2. Highlight clauses that protect the user in one color and the company in another.
3. The student notes the age requirement and what data is collected.
4. Ask, "Does anything here surprise you?"

## Independent Practice / Quick Check (4 minutes)

The student names one privacy law and what it does, lists four components of usage agreements, and identifies one user protection and one company protection from the document.

Look for:
- The law is described accurately from the source.
- Components are found in a real agreement.
- Protections are assigned to the right party.

## Differentiation

**For students needing support**
- Use a plain-language summary.
- Find three components.

**For students ready for more**
- Compare the agreements of two services.
- Find out how to request deletion of data from a service.

## Extensions

- Read the age requirement of a service you want to use.
- Explain to a family member what one agreement says about their data.

## Notes for Parent/Instructor

- Laws differ by place and change. Verify before teaching.
- Reading even one real agreement changes how students treat "I agree."
