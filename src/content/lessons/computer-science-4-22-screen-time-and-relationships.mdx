**Standards covered (2024 Virginia Computer Science SOL)**

- **4.IC.2** The student will describe the impact of screen time on relationships at home and at school.
  - a. Describe the impact of excessive screen time on maintaining friendships and family dynamics.
  - b. Explain how playing video games and the use of social media can impact relationships and personal health.

## Prep

- Write four scenarios, such as a friend who only wants to play online and never in person; a family where everyone uses a phone at dinner; cousins in different states who play a game together every weekend; someone who stays up late gaming and is tired and irritable.

## Warm-up (3 minutes)

Ask, "Have you ever tried to talk to someone who would not look up from a screen? How did that feel?"

## Direct Instruction (5 minutes)

- Define excessive: more than is healthy, so that it crowds out other things.
- Describe effects on friendships and family dynamics, meaning the way a family gets along: less talking, fewer shared activities, arguments about limits, feeling ignored.
- Present video games and social media evenhandedly. They can help: teamwork, keeping in touch, shared interests. They can harm: less sleep and movement, comparing yourself with others, unkind behavior, difficulty stopping.
- Connect to health: sleep, eyes, activity, and mood.

## Guided Practice (5 minutes)

1. The student sorts effects into the Helps and Harms chart.
2. Discuss two scenarios: what is the impact on the relationships?
3. Ask, "What would you suggest to the people in this scenario?"
4. Name signs that screen time has become excessive.

## Independent Practice / Quick Check (4 minutes)

The student explains one way excessive screen time can harm a friendship or family, one way games or social media can help relationships, and one effect on personal health.

Look for:
- Effects on relationships are specific.
- Both positive and negative effects are recognized.
- Health effects are accurate.

## Differentiation

**For students needing support**
- Discuss two scenarios.
- Draw the before and after of a scenario.

**For students ready for more**
- Survey family members about how screens affect time together and summarize the results.
- Propose a family agreement and the reasons for it.

## Extensions

- Plan one screen-free activity with a friend or family member.
- Keep a mood and sleep note for a week alongside screen time.

## Notes for Parent/Instructor

- Many fourth graders do not use social media. Discuss it as something they will meet and already see around them.
- Stay balanced. The standard asks about impact, which includes benefits.
