**Standards covered (2024 Virginia Computer Science SOL)**

- **7.IC.5** The student will evaluate the effect of Artificial Intelligence (AI) in various professions.
  - a. Research AI integration in various professions and evaluate its impact on the job market and society.
  - b. Examine and analyze the impact on job creation and changes in employment needs based on the use of AI.
  - c. Evaluate and explain the benefits and drawbacks of the implementation of AI technologies in various professions.

## Prep

- Bookmark two recent, reliable articles on AI in specific professions, such as medicine, law, journalism, design, customer service, or software development.

## Warm-up (3 minutes)

Ask, "If an AI could do part of a doctor's job, which part would you want it to do? Which part would you never want it to do?"

## Direct Instruction (4 minutes)

- Explain integration: AI is added to a profession's tools and workflow, usually taking on specific tasks.
- Frame the job market analysis: tasks automated, jobs changed, jobs created, and new skills required, such as checking AI output.
- Frame the evaluation. Benefits: speed, consistency, handling large amounts of data, and freeing people for other work. Drawbacks: errors that look convincing, bias, loss of some jobs, over-reliance, privacy, and unclear responsibility when things go wrong.
- Model taking notes on one profession.

## Guided Practice (9 minutes)

1. The student researches two professions and fills in the note sheet.
2. Compare: which has more tasks suited to AI, and why?
3. Identify one job created and one changed in each.
4. Weigh the benefits against the drawbacks for one profession.

## Independent Practice / Quick Check (4 minutes)

The student presents an evaluation of AI in one profession: how it is used, its effect on jobs and needed skills, and a reasoned judgment of benefits and drawbacks.

Look for:
- Research is specific and recent.
- Effects on jobs include both creation and change.
- The evaluation weighs benefits against drawbacks.

## Differentiation

**For students needing support**
- Research one profession.
- List two benefits and two drawbacks.

**For students ready for more**
- Interview a professional about AI in their field.
- Propose guidelines for responsible AI use in one profession.

## Extensions

- Follow AI developments in a profession that interests you.
- Discuss with family how AI affects their work.

## Notes for Parent/Instructor

- Use recent sources and treat predictions as uncertain.
- Who is responsible when an AI-assisted decision is wrong? That question is worth time.
