**Standards covered (2024 Virginia Computer Science SOL)**

- **5.AP.1** The student will apply computational thinking to identify patterns, make use of decomposition to break down problems or processes into sub-components, and design algorithms.
  - a. Identify patterns and repeated steps in an algorithm, problem, or process.
  - b. Decompose a problem or process into a subset of smaller problems or groups of sequential instructions.

## Warm-up (3 minutes)

Ask, "How would you draw a street of ten identical houses? Would you plan each house separately?"

## Direct Instruction (4 minutes)

- Review decomposition: break a problem into sub-problems, then break those into groups of steps that run in order.
- Model with the row of houses: sub-problems are draw one house and move to the next spot. Draw one house breaks into wall, roof, door, window.
- Identify the repeated steps: every house uses the same group of instructions. That group can be written once and reused.
- Explain the payoff: less to write, fewer mistakes, and easier fixes.

## Guided Practice (6 minutes)

1. The student decomposes the bake sale task on sticky notes into sub-problems.
2. Break one sub-problem into sequential steps.
3. Mark the steps or groups that repeat.
4. Rewrite the repeated part once with REPEAT.

## Independent Practice / Quick Check (4 minutes)

Give a new process, such as setting the table for six. The student decomposes it, marks the repeated steps, and rewrites it using a named group of steps and a loop.

Look for:
- Sub-problems cover the whole task.
- Repeated steps are correctly identified.
- The rewritten version is shorter and still complete.

## Differentiation

**For students needing support**
- Use a task with one obvious repeated group.
- Decompose into two levels only.

**For students ready for more**
- Name each group of steps and call it by name in the main algorithm.
- Find a pattern shared by two different problems.

## Extensions

- Decompose a real project the student has coming up.
- Find repeated steps in a recipe or a set of game rules.

## Notes for Parent/Instructor

- Naming a group of steps and reusing it prepares for functions in Grade 6.
- Ask the student to use the words pattern and decompose.
