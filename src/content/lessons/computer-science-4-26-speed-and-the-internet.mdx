**Standards covered (2024 Virginia Computer Science SOL)**

- **4.NI.1** The student will identify the interrelationship between computing devices and a computing network.
  - c. Describe factors that may affect the speed of data transmission.
  - d. Differentiate between networking tasks that require Internet access and tasks that do not require Internet access.

## Prep

- Write twelve task cards, such as typing a story, sending an email, watching a streaming video, playing a downloaded game, printing to a printer in the same house, searching for a fact, taking a photo, video calling a relative, listening to downloaded music, checking the weather forecast, using a calculator, sharing a file with another computer in the house.

## Warm-up (3 minutes)

Ask, "Why does a video sometimes pause and show a spinning circle?"

## Direct Instruction (5 minutes)

- Name factors that affect speed: the amount of data being sent, how many devices are sharing the connection, distance from the router and walls in the way, the type of connection (cable, Wi-Fi, cellular), the speed of the Internet service, and how busy the server is.
- Use the road comparison: more cars and narrower roads mean slower traffic.
- Explain the difference between tasks. Some run entirely on the device. Some need the local network only, such as printing at home. Some need the Internet because the information is on a server elsewhere.
- Give one example of each.

## Guided Practice (6 minutes)

1. The student sorts the task cards and explains three.
2. Find the cards that need a network but not the Internet.
3. Optional: turn on airplane mode and test two tasks.
4. For a slow video call, the student lists three possible causes.

## Independent Practice / Quick Check (4 minutes)

The student names four factors that affect transmission speed and sorts five new tasks you name, explaining how they know.

Look for:
- Factors named are accurate.
- Tasks are sorted correctly with reasoning about where the information is.
- The student distinguishes a local network from the Internet.

## Differentiation

**For students needing support**
- Sort eight cards into two groups.
- Use the road comparison for every factor.

**For students ready for more**
- Test loading speed in two rooms and explain the difference.
- Explain why a downloaded video plays smoothly without a connection.

## Extensions

- List the tasks you did today and mark which needed the Internet.
- Ask what Internet service your family has and what affects it.

## Notes for Parent/Instructor

- The local network versus Internet distinction builds on Grade 3.
- Airplane mode is a simple, safe way to test what works offline.
