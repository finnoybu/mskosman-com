**Standards covered (2024 Virginia Computer Science SOL)**

- **1.CSY.1** The student will describe how computing components work together to create a computing system.
  - a. Identify and define hardware, software, and computing systems.
  - c. Describe how hardware and software work together to form a computing system.

## Prep

- Charge the device.
- Choose two familiar apps or programs to open, such as a drawing app and a camera.

## Warm-up (2 minutes)

Hold up a flashlight or a toy with no batteries, or simply describe one. Ask, "It has all its parts. Why will it not work?" Then say, "A computer needs two things to work too."

## Direct Instruction (6 minutes)

- Define **hardware**: "Hardware is the parts of a computing device you can touch." Touch the screen, the keyboard, the case, the speaker.
- Define **software**: "Software is the programs and apps. Software is the instructions that tell the hardware what to do. You cannot touch software, but you can see what it does."
- Open one app. "This drawing app is software. It tells the screen what to show when I move my finger."
- Define **computing system**: "Hardware and software working together make a computing system."
- Show the teamwork with one action, narrated step by step: "I touch the screen. That is hardware. The app decides what to draw. That is software. The screen shows a line. That is hardware again."
- Make the point both ways: "Hardware with no software does nothing useful. Software with no hardware has nowhere to run."

## Guided Practice (5 minutes)

1. Name items one at a time: keyboard, a game, the screen, the camera app, the speaker, a video player. The student holds up the H or S note for each.
2. Open the second app. Ask, "Which hardware is this software using right now?"
3. Together, tell the story of one action: press, software decides, hardware responds.
4. Ask, "What do we call hardware and software working together?"

## Independent Practice / Quick Check (4 minutes)

Ask the student to:

1. Tell you what hardware is and point to two examples.
2. Tell you what software is and name two examples.
3. Explain how they work together, using one thing they just did on the device.

Look for:
- Correct definitions in the student's own words.
- Examples sorted correctly.
- An explanation that includes both hardware and software.

## Differentiation

**For students needing support**
- Use the test "Can I touch it?" for every item.
- Stay with one app for the whole lesson.
- Use the H and S notes as response cards so the student does not need to say the words at first.

**For students ready for more**
- Ask what would happen if an app were removed from the device, or if the screen were broken.
- Compare the same app on two different devices.

## Extensions

- Draw a device and color the hardware one color. Around it, draw pictures of the software it runs.
- Look at a game console, a smart speaker, or a car dashboard and name its hardware and software.

## Notes for Parent/Instructor

- "You can touch it" is a good first test for hardware. For software, focus on what it does.
- Kindergarten named the parts of a device. This lesson adds the idea that those parts need instructions.
- Use all three terms from the standard: hardware, software, computing system.
