**Standards covered (2024 Virginia Computer Science SOL)**

- **3.IC.3** The student will identify and describe computing technology careers and their impact on society.
  - a. Research computing technology careers.
  - b. Describe the impact careers in computing technology have on society.

## Prep

- Bookmark one or two child-appropriate pages that describe careers such as software developer, cybersecurity analyst, game designer, data analyst, or network technician.
- Prepare the note-taking sheet.

## Warm-up (2 minutes)

Ask, "Who do you think made your favorite app or game? What did they need to know?"

## Direct Instruction (4 minutes)

- Explain: "A computing technology career is a job where creating, running, or protecting computing technology is the main work."
- Give examples with one sentence each: software developers write programs, cybersecurity analysts protect systems, game designers plan how games work, data analysts find patterns in data, and network technicians keep devices connected.
- Model research: read a short section, cover it, and write notes in your own words.
- Explain impact on society: the work these people do changes how others learn, get medical care, travel, shop, and stay safe.

## Guided Practice (8 minutes)

1. The student chooses two careers to research from the bookmarked pages.
2. For each, the student fills in the note-taking sheet in their own words.
3. Ask, "How would life be different if nobody did this job?"
4. The student decides which career has the bigger impact on their own life and why.

## Independent Practice / Quick Check (4 minutes)

The student tells you about one career as a short oral report: what the person does, and how that work affects other people.

Look for:
- Notes are in the student's own words.
- The career described is a computing career, not just a job that uses a computer.
- The impact on society is specific.

## Differentiation

**For students needing support**
- Research one career, with the text read aloud.
- Provide three questions to answer.

**For students ready for more**
- Research a third career and compare the skills needed.
- Find out what someone would study to enter the career.

## Extensions

- Interview someone who works in a computing career.
- Draw a comic of a day in the life of that worker.

## Notes for Parent/Instructor

- Earlier grades covered careers that use technology. Grade 3 turns to careers in computing itself.
- Reuse the cover-and-restate method from Grade 2 research to avoid copying.
