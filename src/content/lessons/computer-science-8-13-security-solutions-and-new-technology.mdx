**Standards covered (2024 Virginia Computer Science SOL)**

- **8.CYB.1** The student will investigate and describe ways to protect sensitive data from malware and other attacks.
  - d. Explore how industries and emerging technologies are addressing cyber solutions.
- **8.CYB.2** The student will investigate and explain how physical and digital security measures can protect electronic information for businesses, governments, and organizations.
  - c. Explore how emerging technologies may affect methods to safeguard personal and public data.

## Prep

- Bookmark two recent, reliable articles: one on how an industry such as banking, health care, or energy defends its systems, and one on an emerging technology affecting security, such as AI for threat detection, biometrics, passkeys, or quantum computing.

## Warm-up (3 minutes)

Ask, "If attackers start using AI, what do defenders need?"

## Direct Instruction (4 minutes)

- Explain industry approaches: dedicated security teams, continuous monitoring, sharing threat information between organizations, required standards and audits, staff training, and incident response plans.
- Explain that emerging technologies cut both ways. They offer new defenses, such as AI spotting unusual activity and sign-in methods that replace passwords. They also create new risks, such as more convincing scams and, in future, computers able to break some current encryption.
- Explain what this means for safeguarding data: methods have to keep changing, and protections for public data such as government records must be updated.

## Guided Practice (8 minutes)

1. The student reads the industry article and notes the approaches used.
2. Read the emerging technology article and fill in the note sheet.
3. Ask, "What does this technology defend against? What new risk does it bring?"
4. Discuss how it could change the way the family's data is protected in ten years.

## Independent Practice / Quick Check (4 minutes)

The student summarizes one industry's approach to cyber threats and explains how one emerging technology may both strengthen and threaten the safeguarding of data.

Look for:
- Industry approaches are specific and sourced.
- The emerging technology is assessed as defense and as risk.
- Effects on personal and public data are considered.

## Differentiation

**For students needing support**
- Research one article.
- Note one defense and one risk.

**For students ready for more**
- Compare the approaches of two industries and explain the difference.
- Research what organizations are doing now to prepare for future threats to encryption.

## Extensions

- Follow security news in one industry for a month.
- Ask an adult how security has changed at their workplace.

## Notes for Parent/Instructor

- This is a fast-moving area. Use recent sources and present predictions as uncertain.
- The lesson joins two sub-items that both concern emerging technology and security.
