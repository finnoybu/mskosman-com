**Standards covered (2024 Virginia Computer Science SOL)**

- **7.AP.4** The student will apply proper attribution when incorporating other sources into original work.
  - a. Apply proper methods of attribution when using work from the Internet and other sources.
  - b. Incorporate information or assets from the Internet into a program with proper attribution.

## Prep

- Identify a source of openly licensed assets or public data and check its license terms.
- Write a checklist: title, creator, source link, license, date accessed, changes made.

## Warm-up (3 minutes)

Ask, "If you used a photo from the Internet in your program and someone asked where it came from, could you tell them? Could you prove you were allowed to use it?"

## Direct Instruction (4 minutes)

- Review why attribution matters: honesty, respect for creators, legal compliance, and letting others find the source.
- Teach the method: record the checklist items at the moment you download something.
- Show where attribution goes in a program: comments at the top of the code, a credits screen or section, and a separate credits file for larger projects.
- Stress checking the license before use: some require credit, some forbid changes or commercial use, and some works may not be used at all.

## Guided Practice (8 minutes)

1. The student finds an asset or data source and checks its license.
2. Record all checklist items.
3. Incorporate it into the program.
4. Add attribution in a comment and in a credits section.

## Independent Practice / Quick Check (4 minutes)

The student incorporates a second item from a different source, attributes it fully, and explains what its license allows and requires.

Look for:
- License terms are checked before use.
- Attribution is complete and placed in the program.
- The student can explain what the license permits.

## Differentiation

**For students needing support**
- Use one asset from a source you have already checked.
- Fill in the checklist together.

**For students ready for more**
- Compare the requirements of two different licenses.
- Attribute a code snippet adapted from a tutorial, noting what was changed.

## Extensions

- Audit an earlier project for missing attributions.
- Create a reusable credits template.

## Notes for Parent/Instructor

- Attribution and permission are separate requirements. Both must be met.
- Recording details at download time is the habit that makes attribution easy.
