**Standards covered (2024 Virginia Computer Science SOL)**

- **7.CYB.1** The student will differentiate physical and digital security measures that protect electronic information.
  - c. Identify common threats and vulnerabilities associated with Internet use and Internet-based systems.
  - d. Identify potential solutions to address common threats and vulnerabilities.

## Prep

- Write threat cards: phishing, malware, ransomware, password guessing, eavesdropping on public Wi-Fi, fake websites, denial of service. Write vulnerability cards: weak or reused passwords, unpatched software, open Wi-Fi, oversharing, no backups, untrained users.
- Write solution cards: strong unique passwords, two-step sign-in, software updates, encryption, backups, firewalls, antivirus tools, user training, verifying links and senders.

## Warm-up (3 minutes)

Ask, "What is the difference between a burglar and an unlocked window?"

## Direct Instruction (5 minutes)

- Define threat: something that could cause harm, such as an attacker or a kind of attack.
- Define vulnerability: a weakness that a threat can use.
- Explain that harm happens when a threat meets a vulnerability, so removing either lowers the risk.
- Go through the threat and vulnerability cards briefly, then show one full match: phishing uses untrained users and reused passwords; solutions are training, verifying senders, and two-step sign-in.

## Guided Practice (7 minutes)

1. The student sorts cards into threats and vulnerabilities.
2. Match each threat to the vulnerabilities it uses.
3. Match solutions to each pairing.
4. Ask, "Which single solution blocks the most threats?"

## Independent Practice / Quick Check (4 minutes)

Give two scenarios, such as a small business with one shared password and no backups. The student identifies the threats and vulnerabilities and proposes solutions.

Look for:
- Threats and vulnerabilities are distinguished.
- Each threat is linked to a weakness.
- Solutions address the specific pairing.

## Differentiation

**For students needing support**
- Work with four threats and four solutions.
- Use the burglar and window comparison for each.

**For students ready for more**
- Rank solutions by how much risk each removes for its effort.
- Research a recent, widely reported incident and identify the threat and vulnerability.

## Extensions

- Check a family account for the vulnerabilities listed.
- Explain two-step sign-in to a relative.

## Notes for Parent/Instructor

- Internet-based systems include websites, cloud services, and connected devices, not only personal computers.
- Keep explanations at the level of what and why. Do not teach how attacks are carried out.
