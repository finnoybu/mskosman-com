**Standards covered (2024 Virginia Computer Science SOL)**

- **6.DA.2** The student will utilize computational tools to visualize and evaluate data.
  - c. Create charts, graphs, models, and simulations to visualize data.
  - d. Describe and synthesize information from a visual representation of data.
- **6.DA.3** The student will make predictions and draw conclusions from data visualizations.
  - a. Visualize data using appropriate graphs, charts, and data visualization techniques to enhance understanding and communicate findings effectively.

## Prep

- Check how to create at least two chart types in your spreadsheet program and how to edit titles, axis labels, and scales.

## Warm-up (2 minutes)

Show a chart with no title or labels. Ask, "What does this show? What is missing?"

## Direct Instruction (4 minutes)

- Review choosing a type that fits the data and the message.
- Teach techniques that improve understanding: a title that states the subject, labeled axes with units, a sensible scale that starts where it should, limited colors used with purpose, and a legend only when needed.
- Define synthesize: combine what several parts of a visualization show into one statement of what it means.
- Model: describe the highest, lowest, and overall shape, then give a one-sentence takeaway.

## Guided Practice (9 minutes)

1. The student creates a first chart from the data and applies the techniques.
2. The student creates a second chart of a different type from the same data.
3. Compare: which communicates the main finding better?
4. The student describes one chart aloud and gives a takeaway.

## Independent Practice / Quick Check (5 minutes)

The student writes a caption of two or three sentences for the better chart that synthesizes what it shows, and explains two design choices made for clarity.

Look for:
- Charts are correctly built and fully labeled.
- Design choices support the message.
- The caption states an overall meaning, not only single values.

## Differentiation

**For students needing support**
- Create one chart and improve it step by step.
- Use the frame: "Overall, the chart shows ___."

**For students ready for more**
- Build a simple model or simulation in the spreadsheet by changing an input and watching the chart update.
- Redesign a confusing published chart.

## Extensions

- Present the chart to someone and ask what they took from it.
- Start a portfolio of visualizations.

## Notes for Parent/Instructor

- Models and simulations are included in the standard. Changing an input cell and watching the chart respond is a simple way in.
- Keep the charts for Lesson 19.
