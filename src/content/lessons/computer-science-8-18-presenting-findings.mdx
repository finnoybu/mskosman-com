**Standards covered (2024 Virginia Computer Science SOL)**

- **8.DA.2** The student will evaluate computational models to analyze patterns and make recommendations or predictions.
  - c. Utilize data visualization techniques to communicate and present findings derived from computational models and simulations.

## Prep

- Arrange for someone to be the audience.
- Check how to make at least two chart types from the model's output.

## Warm-up (3 minutes)

Show a dense table of simulation output. Ask, "If you had 30 seconds to explain this to someone, what would you show them?"

## Direct Instruction (4 minutes)

- State the goal: one clear finding per visual.
- Review choosing a type: line charts for change over time, multiple lines for comparing runs, scatter plots for relationships, and bar charts for comparing final outcomes.
- Teach techniques: a title that states the finding, labeled axes with units, consistent colors across charts, annotations marking key points, and no clutter.
- Teach presenting: the question, the model in brief, the finding with its visual, the limitations, and a recommendation or prediction.

## Guided Practice (9 minutes)

1. The student chooses the two or three most important findings.
2. Create a visualization for each.
3. Add titles that state the finding and annotate key points.
4. Arrange them in a short presentation.

## Independent Practice / Quick Check (4 minutes)

The student presents to the audience in about three minutes, then answers one question and notes one improvement to make.

Look for:
- Each visualization communicates one finding.
- Techniques are applied correctly.
- Limitations are stated.

## Differentiation

**For students needing support**
- Present one finding with one chart.
- Use a presentation template.

**For students ready for more**
- Create an interactive version where the audience can change an input.
- Present the same findings for two different audiences and adjust.

## Extensions

- Share the presentation with a wider audience.
- Critique a data presentation from a news source.

## Notes for Parent/Instructor

- A chart title that states the finding does half the communicating.
- Presenting limitations builds trust. Encourage it.
