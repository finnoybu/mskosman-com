**Standards covered (2024 Virginia Computer Science SOL)**

- **1.AP.2** The student will plan and implement algorithms that include the use of sequence and an event based on a predetermined task.
  - a. Plan and create a design document that illustrates thoughts, ideas, and stories in a sequential manner.

## Prep

- Fold or rule two sheets of paper into four boxes each and number the boxes 1 to 4.
- Choose a short story the student knows well.

## Warm-up (2 minutes)

Ask, "If we wanted to build a blanket fort, what would we need to decide before we start?" Guide the student to the idea of planning first.

## Direct Instruction (5 minutes)

- Explain: "A design document is a plan on paper. It shows the steps in order before we do them."
- Show the four numbered boxes. "The numbers tell us the order."
- Model with the familiar story. Before drawing, plan aloud: "What happens first? Then what? What is the last thing?" Touch each box as you decide.
- Draw one quick sketch in each box. Add a word or short label under each.
- Read the finished plan from box 1 to box 4. Ask, "Could someone who has never heard this story follow my plan?"

## Guided Practice (6 minutes)

1. Choose a task with four steps together, such as planting a seed (dig a hole, put in the seed, cover it, water it).
2. Plan first. The student says the four steps while pointing to the four empty boxes.
3. The student draws each step. Help with labels as needed.
4. Check the plan together: is anything missing or out of order?

## Independent Practice / Quick Check (5 minutes)

On a fresh sheet, the student plans and draws their own idea or story in four steps, then explains it to you from box 1 to box 4.

Look for:
- The student decides the steps before drawing.
- Four boxes show four different steps in an order that makes sense.
- The student can explain the plan in sequence.

## Differentiation

**For students needing support**
- Use three boxes.
- Give picture cards to arrange and glue in place of drawing.
- Plan aloud together and write the labels for the student.

**For students ready for more**
- Use six boxes.
- Write a short sentence under each box in place of a label.

## Extensions

- Make a storyboard for a real project, such as making a card for someone, and then follow it.
- Storyboard the morning routine and hang it where it will be used.

## Notes for Parent/Instructor

- The verb in the standard is "plan and create." The spoken planning step before any drawing is part of the skill.
- This builds on the three-part plans from Kindergarten by adding more steps and numbers.
- Keep these storyboards. A plan from this lesson can be reused in Lessons 5 and 6.
