**Standards covered (2024 Virginia Computer Science SOL)**

- **5.IC.1** The student will analyze the impact of inappropriate use of computing technologies.
  - a. Predict consequences for inappropriate uses of computing technologies.
  - b. Describe how technology-related problems can be avoided or prevented.
  - c. Develop solutions for a scenario involving inappropriate use of computing technologies.

## Prep

- Write five scenarios with the outcome left open, such as a student posts a joke about a classmate; someone uses the same password everywhere; a group copies a project from a website; a student clicks a link in an unexpected message; someone records a friend without asking.

## Warm-up (3 minutes)

Read one scenario. Ask, "What do you think happens next? And after that?"

## Direct Instruction (4 minutes)

- Explain predicting consequences: think about the short term and the long term, and about everyone affected, including the person who acted.
- Note that online actions can be permanent and can spread.
- Explain prevention: most problems can be avoided by pausing before acting, asking permission, using safeguards, and knowing the rules.
- Explain developing a solution: repair the harm, stop it spreading, tell the right person, and change the habit.

## Guided Practice (6 minutes)

1. The student predicts consequences for two scenarios, short and long term.
2. For each, identify what would have prevented the problem.
3. Choose one scenario and develop a full solution together.
4. Ask, "Who would need to be involved for that solution to work?"

## Independent Practice / Quick Check (5 minutes)

The student takes a new scenario through all four columns on their own and presents the solution.

Look for:
- Consequences include effects on others and on the future.
- Prevention is specific.
- The solution addresses repair and prevention.

## Differentiation

**For students needing support**
- Work through two scenarios with the chart.
- Act out the scenario and its consequences.

**For students ready for more**
- Write a scenario with a difficult tradeoff.
- Compare two solutions and argue for the better one.

## Extensions

- Write a short guide: "Before you post, ask yourself..."
- Discuss a news story about a technology mistake.

## Notes for Parent/Instructor

- Fifth graders can think several steps ahead. Keep asking "and then what?"
- Include how the person who caused the problem can make it right.
