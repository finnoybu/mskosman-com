**Standards covered (2024 Virginia Computer Science SOL)**

- **6.CSY.2** The student will identify and explain hardware, software, and connectivity problems and troubleshooting solutions.
  - a. Identify and explain hardware, software, and connectivity problems and solutions with accurate terminology.
  - b. Identify resources for troubleshooting hardware, software, and connectivity-related problems.

## Prep

- Write six problem cards across the three categories.
- Find the official help or support page for one of your devices or programs and bookmark it.

## Warm-up (3 minutes)

Ask, "When you cannot fix something yourself, where do you look? How do you know that source is trustworthy?"

## Direct Instruction (4 minutes)

- Review the three categories and the accurate terms for describing them.
- Model a full explanation: the problem, its likely cause, and a solution. "The laptop is connected to Wi-Fi but pages will not load. The router may have lost its connection. Restarting the router should restore it."
- Introduce resources: built-in help and settings diagnostics, the maker's official support pages, user manuals, a knowledgeable person, and community forums.
- Explain evaluating resources: official sources first, and be wary of sites that ask you to download a tool or pay.

## Guided Practice (7 minutes)

1. The student explains three problem cards: category, likely cause, and solution.
2. Search the bookmarked support page for one of the problems.
3. Compare what it says with the student's proposed solution.
4. Start a resource list with where to look for each category.

## Independent Practice / Quick Check (4 minutes)

The student explains two new problems in full and names the best resource for each, with a reason.

Look for:
- Explanations include cause and solution in accurate terms.
- Resources named are appropriate and trustworthy.
- The student shows caution about unofficial sources.

## Differentiation

**For students needing support**
- Explain two cards using a sentence frame.
- Use one resource: the official support page.

**For students ready for more**
- Write a help article for a problem your family has had.
- Compare the advice of two resources on the same problem.

## Extensions

- Complete the resource list and keep it with the family's devices.
- Help someone solve a problem using an official support page.

## Notes for Parent/Instructor

- Knowing where to look is the new skill this year.
- Supervise searches. Many results for technical problems are advertisements or unsafe downloads.
