**Standards covered (2024 Virginia Computer Science SOL)**

- **K.CSY.1** The student will identify the purpose and components of a computing device.
  - c. Identify different types of computing devices with screens.
  - d. Identify tasks and activities at home and at school that use screens.

## Prep

- Collect or draw pictures of six devices with screens.
- Draw a simple house on one sheet of paper and a simple school on another.

## Warm-up (3 minutes)

Ask, "What does a screen do?" (It shows pictures and words.) Then go on a one-minute screen hunt in the room or home: the student points to every screen they can find.

## Direct Instruction (4 minutes)

- Lay out the device pictures. Name each one and have the student repeat the name.
- Explain: "These are all computing devices with screens. They look different because they are used in different ways."
- Give one difference for each in simple terms: a smartphone fits in a pocket, a laptop folds, a desktop computer stays on a desk, a smartwatch is worn on the wrist.
- Shift to tasks: "People use screens to do things." Name one home example (a video call with a grandparent) and one learning example (reading a story on a tablet).
- Place a mark or quick drawing for each example on the house sheet or the school sheet.

## Guided Practice (5 minutes)

1. Hold up a device picture. The student names it.
2. Ask, "What is one thing someone does with this at home?" Add it to the house sheet.
3. Ask, "What is one thing someone does with a screen for school or learning?" Add it to the school sheet.
4. Repeat until each sheet has at least three tasks.

## Independent Practice / Quick Check (3 minutes)

Mix the pictures. The student names each device as you show it. Then ask the student to tell you two things screens are used for at home and two things screens are used for at school or during lessons.

Look for:
- The student names at least three types of devices with screens.
- The student gives at least two home tasks and two school or learning tasks.

## Differentiation

**For students needing support**
- Use real devices from the home in place of pictures where possible.
- Start with three devices.
- Offer choices: "Do we use this to watch a show or to cook dinner?"

**For students ready for more**
- Find screens outside the home, such as at a store checkout or in a car, and say what each is used for.
- Sort the device pictures by an attribute, such as size or whether they are carried or stay in place.

## Extensions

- Keep a one-day tally of the screens the family uses and what each was used for.
- Draw a picture of a favorite screen activity and one favorite activity that uses no screen.

## Notes for Parent/Instructor

- For homeschool and tutoring, treat "school" as wherever and whenever learning happens. If the student has attended a classroom, use those memories too.
- Keep the tone neutral. This lesson is about noticing how screens are used. Healthy habits are covered in Lesson 13.
- This lesson reuses the sorting idea from Lessons 1 and 2, which is a good chance to say "attribute" again.
