**Standards covered (2024 Virginia Computer Science SOL)**

- **2.CYB.1** The student will model safe and responsible behaviors when using information and computing technologies.
  - c. Demonstrate and model safe and responsible behaviors when using computing technologies and online communication.
- **2.IC.1** The student will examine the positive and negative impacts of how using computing technologies has changed the way people live, work, and interact.
  - b. Compare and contrast appropriate and inappropriate online behaviors that apply in the physical environment and the online environment.

## Prep

- Write eight behavior cards, such as taking turns, saying something unkind, sharing someone's secret, asking before borrowing, interrupting, giving a compliment, pretending to be someone else, including others.
- Arrange for a family member to receive a short message.

## Warm-up (3 minutes)

Ask, "What are our rules for how we treat people at the park or at the table?" Then ask, "Do those rules change when we are on a screen?"

## Direct Instruction (5 minutes)

- State the idea: "The physical environment is the world around us. The online environment is where we connect through devices. Good behavior belongs in both."
- Take one card, such as saying something unkind. Describe what it looks like in person and what it looks like online. Decide whether it is appropriate or inappropriate in each place.
- Point out a difference: online, words can be saved and shared, and you cannot see the other person's face.
- Name the behaviors to model online: be kind, be honest about who you are, protect private information, ask before sharing, and tell a grown-up when something is wrong.

## Guided Practice (7 minutes)

1. For four cards, the student describes the in-person version and the online version and fills in the chart.
2. Ask, "What is the same? What is different?"
3. The student writes a short message to the family member and checks it against the list of behaviors before sending.
4. Role-play receiving an unkind message. The student models what to do.

## Independent Practice / Quick Check (4 minutes)

The student compares two more cards on their own. Then the student shows you, on the device, how they would respond to a friendly message and what they check first.

Look for:
- The student identifies the same behavior as appropriate or inappropriate in both places.
- The student names at least one way online behavior is different.
- The student's own actions on the device are safe and responsible.

## Differentiation

**For students needing support**
- Use four cards and act out each one.
- Send a drawing or voice message in place of typed words.

**For students ready for more**
- Discuss why people sometimes behave worse online than in person.
- Write three tips for a younger child about behaving well online.

## Extensions

- Make a poster titled "Same rules, both places."
- Notice and praise good online behavior by family members.

## Notes for Parent/Instructor

- This lesson joins two standards that overlap: modeling safe behavior and comparing behavior in the physical and online environments.
- You stay with the student for all online communication.
