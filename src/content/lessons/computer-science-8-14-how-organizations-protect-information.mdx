**Standards covered (2024 Virginia Computer Science SOL)**

- **8.CYB.2** The student will investigate and explain how physical and digital security measures can protect electronic information for businesses, governments, and organizations.
  - a. Investigate and explain how physical and digital security measures are used to safeguard electronic information.
  - b. Research the advantages and limitations of different security measures in protecting users against security threats.

## Prep

- Bookmark a plain-language overview of organizational security practices from a government or educational source.
- Choose an organization the student knows something about.

## Warm-up (3 minutes)

Ask, "What information does a hospital hold? Who would want it, and what would stop them?"

## Direct Instruction (5 minutes)

- Describe physical measures in organizations: controlled entry, locked server rooms, cameras, visitor logs, secure disposal of drives and paper, and protection from fire and power loss.
- Describe digital measures: access control by role, two-step sign-in, encryption of stored and transmitted data, firewalls, monitoring and logs, backups, and regular updates.
- Explain defense in depth: several layers, so that one failure does not expose everything.
- Explain advantages and limitations: every measure has costs, can be bypassed, or depends on people using it properly.

## Guided Practice (8 minutes)

1. The student lists the kinds of information the chosen organization must protect.
2. Read the source and fill in the table for six measures.
3. For each, research or reason out one advantage and one limitation.
4. Identify a threat that only a combination of measures would stop.

## Independent Practice / Quick Check (4 minutes)

The student explains how the chosen organization could protect its information with at least three physical and three digital measures, giving an advantage and a limitation for two of them.

Look for:
- Both types of measures are explained.
- Advantages and limitations are specific.
- Layering is understood.

## Differentiation

**For students needing support**
- Complete the table for four measures.
- Use a familiar organization such as a library.

**For students ready for more**
- Research a law or standard that requires an industry to protect certain data.
- Design a layered plan for a small business with a limited budget.

## Extensions

- Notice the security measures the next time you visit a bank, clinic, or office.
- Ask an adult what measures their workplace uses, in general terms.

## Notes for Parent/Instructor

- Grade 7 compared the two kinds of security. Grade 8 applies them to organizations.
- People are both a limitation and a strength of every measure.
