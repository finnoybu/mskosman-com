**Standards covered (2024 Virginia Computer Science SOL)**

- **4.CYB.1** The student will examine the impacts of appropriate and inappropriate use of computing technologies.
  - a. Examine and explain scenarios for appropriate and inappropriate use of computing technologies.
  - b. Develop possible solutions involving inappropriate use of computing technologies.

## Prep

- Write six short scenarios, such as a student shares a classmate's photo without asking; someone uses a group chat to leave one person out; a student helps a grandparent set up a video call; someone downloads a game from an unknown site; a student reports a mean comment; someone guesses a sibling's password.

## Warm-up (3 minutes)

Read one scenario aloud. Ask, "Who is affected here? How?"

## Direct Instruction (4 minutes)

- Explain examining a scenario: what happened, whether the use was appropriate, who was affected, and what the impact was.
- Note that impacts can be on people, on devices and information, and on trust.
- Explain developing a solution: what could fix the harm now, and what could prevent it next time.
- Model with one card, filling in all three columns.

## Guided Practice (6 minutes)

1. The student examines three scenarios and fills in the chart.
2. For each inappropriate use, the student proposes a solution.
3. Ask, "Would that solution work? Who would need to agree?"
4. Identify what made the appropriate scenarios appropriate.

## Independent Practice / Quick Check (4 minutes)

The student examines the remaining scenarios on their own and presents one solution in detail.

Look for:
- Each scenario is judged with a reason.
- Impacts on other people are identified.
- Solutions address both repair and prevention.

## Differentiation

**For students needing support**
- Examine three scenarios.
- Act out a scenario before discussing it.

**For students ready for more**
- Write a scenario with no clear answer and argue both sides.
- Propose a family or classroom rule that would prevent one of the problems.

## Extensions

- Notice a real example of positive technology use this week and describe its impact.
- Write an advice column answer to one scenario.

## Notes for Parent/Instructor

- Include appropriate scenarios so the lesson is not only about what goes wrong.
- Keep scenarios realistic for a nine- or ten-year-old.
