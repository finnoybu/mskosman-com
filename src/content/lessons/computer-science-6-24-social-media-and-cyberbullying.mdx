**Standards covered (2024 Virginia Computer Science SOL)**

- **6.IC.2** The student will analyze the impact of screen time on physical and mental health.
  - d. Discuss the potential impact the use of social media may have on self-identity and mental health.
  - e. Define cyberbullying and its impact on one’s health and well-being.
  - f. Discuss the possible effects of cyberbullying.
  - g. Identify ways to report illegal or psychologically maladaptive online behavior.

## Prep

- Write three scenarios: someone comparing themselves with edited photos; a group chat where one person is repeatedly mocked; a stranger pressuring someone to send personal photos.
- Find out how reporting works on a platform your family uses and who to contact for serious matters.

## Warm-up (3 minutes)

Ask, "Do people show their whole lives online, or only parts? How might that affect someone looking at it?"

## Direct Instruction (6 minutes)

- Define self-identity: how a person sees and describes themselves.
- Discuss social media's possible effects: connection and belonging on one side; comparison with curated images, pressure for likes, and fear of missing out on the other. These can affect mood, confidence, and sleep.
- Define cyberbullying: "Using technology to repeatedly and deliberately harass, threaten, embarrass, or exclude someone."
- Discuss its effects: anxiety, sadness, isolation, trouble sleeping and concentrating, avoiding school or activities, and effects that last because content can spread and stay.
- Teach reporting: do not respond or retaliate, save evidence, block the person, report it in the platform, and tell a trusted adult. Illegal behavior, such as threats or requests for inappropriate images, should go to an adult right away, who can contact the platform, the school, or law enforcement.

## Guided Practice (6 minutes)

1. Discuss the first scenario: what is the effect on self-identity, and what would help?
2. Discuss the second: is it cyberbullying, and what are the possible effects?
3. Discuss the third: why is this an emergency, and who should be told?
4. Begin a personal reporting plan listing three trusted adults and the steps.

## Independent Practice / Quick Check (4 minutes)

The student defines cyberbullying, describes two effects on well-being, explains one way social media can shape self-identity, and completes the reporting plan.

Look for:
- The definition includes repeated and deliberate harm through technology.
- Effects on health and well-being are described.
- The plan names specific adults and steps.

## Differentiation

**For students needing support**
- Discuss one scenario at a time over two sessions.
- Use a printed step list for reporting.

**For students ready for more**
- Write advice for someone who witnesses cyberbullying.
- Examine how a platform's design encourages comparison.

## Extensions

- Review privacy and reporting tools on a platform with an adult.
- Discuss as a family what to do if something upsetting happens online.

## Notes for Parent/Instructor

- These topics can be personal. Let the student know they can talk to you without losing their devices.
- "Psychologically maladaptive" in the standard refers to online behavior that harms mental health. Plain words work better with students.
