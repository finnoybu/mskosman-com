**Standards covered (2024 Virginia Computer Science SOL)**

- **6.IC.6** The student will investigate and analyze the impact of the progression and advancement of AI technologies on industries.
  - a. Discuss the type of industries that may be impacted by the use and integration of Artificial Intelligence (AI).
  - b. Compare and contrast the evolving nature of work across diverse industries because of the progression and advancement of Artificial Intelligence.

## Prep

- For each industry, note one current use of AI from a reliable recent source.
- Draw a chart: Industry, How AI is used, Tasks changing, New tasks or jobs.

## Warm-up (3 minutes)

Ask, "Which jobs do you think AI could change the most? Which the least? Why?"

## Direct Instruction (5 minutes)

- Explain how AI enters an industry: automating routine tasks, assisting people with decisions, and making new products and services possible.
- Go through the industry cards with your examples.
- Explain how work evolves: some tasks are automated, some jobs change so that people work alongside AI, and new jobs appear, such as building, checking, and maintaining AI systems.
- Introduce comparison questions: How much of the work is routine? How much depends on human judgment, care, or creativity? How serious are mistakes?

## Guided Practice (6 minutes)

1. The student fills in the chart for three industries.
2. Compare two industries: what is alike and different in how work is changing?
3. Ask, "Where should a person always make the final decision?"
4. Identify skills that stay valuable across industries.

## Independent Practice / Quick Check (4 minutes)

The student names four industries affected by AI, compares how work is changing in two of them, and identifies two skills that will remain important.

Look for:
- Industries and uses are realistic.
- The comparison includes a similarity and a difference.
- Human skills are identified.

## Differentiation

**For students needing support**
- Chart two industries.
- Use one familiar job in each.

**For students ready for more**
- Research how one job has changed in the last five years.
- Debate whether a particular decision should ever be automated.

## Extensions

- Ask adults in different industries whether AI has changed their work.
- Follow news on AI in one industry for a month.

## Notes for Parent/Instructor

- This field changes quickly. Use recent sources and present forecasts as uncertain.
- Balance concern about jobs lost with attention to jobs changed and created.
