**Standards covered (2024 Virginia Computer Science SOL)**

- **7.IC.1** The student will assess the national and global impact of computing technologies.
  - c. Evaluate the environmental impact of computing technologies nationally and globally.

## Prep

- Find two current, reliable sources: one on the energy and water used by data centers or the materials used in devices, and one on electronic waste or on technology used to help the environment. Check the figures.

## Warm-up (3 minutes)

Hold up an old phone or device. Ask, "Where did the materials in this come from? Where will it go when nobody wants it?"

## Direct Instruction (5 minutes)

- Describe the life cycle of a device: mining materials, manufacturing, shipping, use, and disposal.
- Describe impacts across it: energy and water use in manufacturing and in data centers, emissions from electricity generation, and electronic waste containing hazardous and valuable materials.
- Describe benefits: computing also helps the environment through monitoring, modeling climate, improving efficiency in transport and buildings, and reducing travel.
- Explain evaluating: weigh costs against benefits, consider who bears the costs and where, and consider what can be changed.

## Guided Practice (7 minutes)

1. The student reads the first source and records impacts with figures.
2. Read the second source and record benefits or responses.
3. Fill in the evaluation chart.
4. Ask, "Which impacts fall on other countries or communities?"

## Independent Practice / Quick Check (4 minutes)

The student writes an evaluation of one computing technology's environmental impact, citing evidence for costs and benefits, and proposes one action for individuals and one for organizations.

Look for:
- Impacts across the life cycle are identified.
- Evidence is cited from the sources.
- Both costs and benefits are weighed.

## Differentiation

**For students needing support**
- Focus on electronic waste.
- Use one source.

**For students ready for more**
- Estimate the family's electronic waste over ten years.
- Research how one company reports its environmental impact and assess the claims.

## Extensions

- Find out how to recycle electronics properly in your area.
- Extend the life of a device by repairing or repurposing it.

## Notes for Parent/Instructor

- Figures on energy and waste change. Use current sources and cite them.
- Keep both sides in view. Computing is both a cause of harm and a tool for solutions.
