**Standards covered (2024 Virginia Computer Science SOL)**

- **3.CYB.3** The student will define and explain cybersecurity.
  - a. Define cybersecurity.
  - b. Research and identify problems and consequences related to inappropriate use of computing devices and networks.
  - c. Model safe and responsible behaviors when using computing technologies and online communication.

## Prep

- Find and bookmark a short, child-appropriate article or page about staying safe online.
- Think of two real, mild examples of technology misuse you can share.

## Warm-up (3 minutes)

Ask, "What do we do to keep our home secure?" List the ideas. Then ask, "What needs protecting on a computer?"

## Direct Instruction (5 minutes)

- Define: "Cybersecurity is protecting devices, networks, and information from being damaged, stolen, or used without permission."
- Connect it to earlier lessons: passwords, private information, and authentication are all cybersecurity.
- Name problems that come from inappropriate use: clicking unknown links, downloading without asking, sharing passwords, being unkind online, using someone else's account.
- Link each to a consequence: a device infected with harmful software, stolen information, lost work, hurt feelings, loss of trust or privileges.

## Guided Practice (7 minutes)

1. Read the bookmarked article together. The student finds two problems it describes.
2. Record each problem and its consequence in the chart.
3. Add one problem from your own examples.
4. The student demonstrates on the device: what they do before clicking a link, and how they close something they did not expect.

## Independent Practice / Quick Check (4 minutes)

The student defines cybersecurity in their own words, gives two problems with their consequences, and shows one safe behavior on the device.

Look for:
- The definition includes protecting devices, networks, or information.
- Each problem is matched with a realistic consequence.
- The student's own behavior on the device is safe.

## Differentiation

**For students needing support**
- Read the article aloud and pause after each paragraph.
- Use picture cards for problems and consequences and match them.

**For students ready for more**
- Research a second source and compare what the two say.
- Write three cybersecurity tips for younger children.

## Extensions

- Make a cybersecurity poster for the family computer area.
- Add a rule to the family technology rules based on what was learned.

## Notes for Parent/Instructor

- "Research" at this grade means reading a source you have chosen and pulling out information.
- Keep examples calm and practical. The goal is good habits, not fear.
