**Standards covered (2024 Virginia Computer Science SOL)**

- **7.IC.2** The student will describe and explain the impact of screen time on interactions with others.
  - b. Research the type of data collected on social media and online platforms that monitor social interactions.
  - d. Create a social media usage plan that demonstrates safe practices, meaningful use, and a balanced approach.

## Prep

- Choose one platform the student uses or is likely to use and open its privacy summary or data settings page.
- Draft plan headings: Safe practices, Meaningful use, Balance.

## Warm-up (3 minutes)

Ask, "A platform is free to use. So how does the company make money?"

## Direct Instruction (5 minutes)

- Explain the types of data platforms collect: what you post, who you connect with and message, what you like and comment on, how long you look at things, your location and device, and information from contacts.
- Explain why: to show content that keeps people engaged, to target advertising, and to improve the service.
- Introduce the three parts of a usage plan. Safe practices: privacy settings, strong sign-in, limited personal information, and knowing how to block and report. Meaningful use: purposes you choose, such as keeping in touch or learning. Balance: time limits, screen-free times, and offline activities.
- Model one entry in each part.

## Guided Practice (8 minutes)

1. The student reads the platform's data summary and lists five types of data collected.
2. Ask, "Which of these surprised you? Which could you limit in settings?"
3. The student drafts the plan under the three headings.
4. Review it together and make it specific.

## Independent Practice / Quick Check (4 minutes)

The student finalizes the plan with at least three specific commitments under each heading and explains how the research on data collection shaped it.

Look for:
- Types of data collected are drawn from the source.
- The plan addresses safety, meaningful use, and balance.
- Commitments are specific and realistic.

## Differentiation

**For students needing support**
- List three types of data.
- Write one commitment under each heading.

**For students ready for more**
- Compare the data practices of two platforms.
- Review and adjust the privacy settings of a real account with an adult.

## Extensions

- Revisit the plan after a month and revise it.
- Share the plan with a parent and agree on check-ins.

## Notes for Parent/Instructor

- Check platform age requirements. A plan for future use is a valid outcome.
- A specific plan can be kept. "Use it less" cannot.
