**Standards covered (2024 Virginia Computer Science SOL)**

- **2.CSY.1** The student will describe the characteristics of computing systems including hardware, software, input, and output.
  - a. Describe how hardware and software work together to accomplish a task.
  - b. Define and categorize components as inputs and outputs.
  - c. Describe how a computing system receives input and provides output.

## Prep

- Make the component cards with a small drawing on each.
- Open a drawing or note-taking app on the device.

## Warm-up (3 minutes)

Ask the student to clap when you say "go." Say it. Ask, "What went into your ears? What came out of your hands?"

## Direct Instruction (5 minutes)

- Define: "Input is information that goes into a computing system. Output is information that comes out of it."
- Give examples of input components: keyboard, mouse, microphone, camera. Output components: screen, speaker, printer.
- Point out that a touchscreen does both: it takes in touches and shows pictures.
- Trace one task on the device: "I press a key. That is input, through hardware. The software decides which letter it is. The letter appears on the screen. That is output, through hardware."
- Summarize: hardware takes in the input, software processes it, and hardware gives the output.

## Guided Practice (6 minutes)

1. The student sorts the component cards under Input and Output and explains two choices.
2. Discuss where the touchscreen card belongs.
3. On the device, the student does one action and tells its story: input, software, output.
4. Repeat with a different input, such as speaking into the microphone to record.

## Independent Practice / Quick Check (4 minutes)

Name three tasks: taking a photo, playing a song, typing a name. For each, the student names the input, the output, and the part software plays.

Look for:
- Components are categorized correctly, with the touchscreen recognized as both.
- The student defines input and output.
- Each explanation includes hardware and software.

## Differentiation

**For students needing support**
- Use the frame: "The input is ___. The output is ___."
- Sort only six cards.

**For students ready for more**
- Find input and output on a device with no screen, such as a smart speaker or a microwave.
- Draw a diagram with arrows showing input, processing, and output.

## Extensions

- Go on an input and output hunt around the home.
- Play a game where one person names an output and the other guesses the input that caused it.

## Notes for Parent/Instructor

- Grade 1 introduced hardware and software. Input and output describe the direction information travels.
- "Processing" is a helpful word for the software step, though the standard does not require it.
