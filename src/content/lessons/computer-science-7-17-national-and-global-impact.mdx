**Standards covered (2024 Virginia Computer Science SOL)**

- **7.IC.1** The student will assess the national and global impact of computing technologies.
  - a. Discuss specific examples of how computing technologies have influenced various national and global industries and sectors.
  - b. Analyze the implications of emerging technologies and potential real-world impact nationally and globally.

## Prep

- Bookmark one article on computing's effect on an industry, such as agriculture, shipping, finance, or medicine, and one on an emerging technology with global reach.

## Warm-up (3 minutes)

Ask, "Pick an object in this room. How many countries and computer systems do you think were involved in getting it here?"

## Direct Instruction (4 minutes)

- Define sector: a broad part of the economy, such as health, energy, transportation, finance, or agriculture.
- Give specific examples: logistics systems that track containers worldwide, electronic payment networks, satellite navigation in farming and transport, and medical imaging.
- Explain analyzing implications at two scales: national and global. Consider jobs, trade, security, access, and inequality between places.
- Model one implication chain: a technology, its direct effect, and a second effect that follows.

## Guided Practice (8 minutes)

1. The student reads the industry article and notes specific examples.
2. Mark on the map the places connected by that industry's systems.
3. Read about the emerging technology and list national and global implications.
4. Build one implication chain.

## Independent Practice / Quick Check (4 minutes)

The student presents two specific examples of computing's influence on industries and an analysis of one emerging technology's implications at national and global scale.

Look for:
- Examples are specific and sourced.
- Implications are considered at both scales.
- Second-order effects are identified.

## Differentiation

**For students needing support**
- Research one article.
- List two implications.

**For students ready for more**
- Compare how two countries are affected differently by the same technology.
- Identify who makes decisions about a global technology.

## Extensions

- Trace the supply chain of a product you own.
- Follow one global technology story for a month.

## Notes for Parent/Instructor

- Grade 6 looked at the local community. Grade 7 widens to the nation and the world.
- Ask for specifics. "Technology changed everything" is not an answer.
