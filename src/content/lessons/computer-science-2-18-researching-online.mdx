**Standards covered (2024 Virginia Computer Science SOL)**

- **2.NI.1** The student will demonstrate the use of the Internet in gathering information to accomplish a task.
  - a. Explore ways information is organized and shared on the Internet.
  - b. Gather information from the Internet.
  - c. Summarize collected information using own words.

## Prep

- Choose a research task, such as finding three facts about an animal for a short report.
- Preview the websites and bookmark them.

## Warm-up (3 minutes)

Ask, "If the Internet has millions of pages, how does anyone find the one they need?"

## Direct Instruction (5 minutes)

- Explain organization: information is kept on websites. A website has pages, and pages have headings, menus, links, pictures, and a search box that help you find things.
- Explain sharing: people and organizations put information on websites so others can read it. Links connect one page to another.
- Model gathering: open a bookmarked site, use the menu or search box, and read one short section aloud.
- Model summarizing: close or cover the screen. Say the idea in your own words. Then write one sentence.
- Explain why: "Using our own words shows we understand, and it is honest. Copying someone else's sentences is not our work."

## Guided Practice (7 minutes)

1. Explore one page together. The student points to the heading, a link, the menu, and the search box.
2. The student uses the site to find the first fact.
3. Cover the screen. The student says the fact in their own words, then writes it.
4. Repeat for a second fact.

## Independent Practice / Quick Check (5 minutes)

The student gathers a third fact with little help and writes it in their own words. Then ask, "How is information organized on a website? How did you find what you needed?"

Look for:
- The student uses headings, links, or search to locate information.
- The gathered facts fit the task.
- Summaries are in the student's own words, not copied.

## Differentiation

**For students needing support**
- Read the text aloud to the student and have them retell it before writing.
- Gather one fact only.

**For students ready for more**
- Compare two websites on the same topic and say which was easier to use.
- Record where each fact came from.

## Extensions

- Turn the three facts into a short report or poster.
- Research a question from the family wonder list.

## Notes for Parent/Instructor

- You choose the sites. Open searching is for later grades.
- Covering the screen before summarizing is the simplest way to prevent copying.
