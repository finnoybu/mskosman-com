**Standards covered (2024 Virginia Computer Science SOL)**

- **5.IC.3** The student will identify the impact of computing technologies on the workforce, culture, and global society.
  - a. Research and analyze computing technology careers in global society.
  - b. Explore the impact of emerging technologies on workforce, culture, and global society.

## Prep

- Bookmark one page about a computing career that involves people working across countries, and one child-appropriate article about an emerging technology such as artificial intelligence, robotics, or self-driving vehicles.
- Prepare a note sheet: What it is, Where it is used, Who it affects.

## Warm-up (3 minutes)

Ask, "How could five people in five countries build one app together?"

## Direct Instruction (4 minutes)

- Explain global society: people and economies around the world are connected, and technology is a main reason.
- Describe computing careers in that setting: teams spread across countries, products used worldwide, and the need to work across languages and time zones.
- Define emerging technology: new technology that is still developing and spreading.
- Frame three areas to look for: effects on the workforce (jobs created, changed, or replaced), on culture (how people communicate and create), and on global society (who benefits and who is left out).

## Guided Practice (8 minutes)

1. The student researches the career and notes how it connects to other countries. Mark them on the map.
2. The student reads about the emerging technology and takes notes.
3. Sort the effects found into workforce, culture, and global society.
4. Ask, "Who benefits most? Who might be harmed or left out?"

## Independent Practice / Quick Check (4 minutes)

The student gives a two-minute spoken summary: one computing career in a global setting, and one emerging technology with an effect on each of the three areas.

Look for:
- Research is restated in the student's own words.
- The global aspect of the career is explained.
- Effects are identified for workforce, culture, and global society.

## Differentiation

**For students needing support**
- Research the emerging technology only.
- Read the article together and pause to take notes.

**For students ready for more**
- Compare how the same technology is used in two countries.
- Predict a job that might exist in twenty years and the skills it would need.

## Extensions

- Ask an adult how their work connects with people in other places.
- Follow one emerging technology in the news for a month.

## Notes for Parent/Instructor

- Choose current sources. Emerging technology changes quickly.
- Encourage a balanced view of benefits and harms.
