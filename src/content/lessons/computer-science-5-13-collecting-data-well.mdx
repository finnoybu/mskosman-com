**Standards covered (2024 Virginia Computer Science SOL)**

- **5.DA.1** The student will collect data or use data sets to solve a problem or investigate a topic.
  - a. Identify accurate ways data can be collected.
  - b. Evaluate the reliability of the data source.

## Prep

- Prepare two sources that give different numbers for the same fact, such as a reference book or official site and an unsigned web post or advertisement.

## Warm-up (3 minutes)

Ask three people to estimate the length of the table, then measure it. Ask, "Which number would you trust in a report? Why?"

## Direct Instruction (5 minutes)

- List ways to collect data: measuring with a tool, counting, observing and recording, surveying, running an experiment, and using an existing data set.
- Explain what makes collection accurate: the right tool, the same method each time, repeated measurements, recording immediately, and enough samples.
- Explain bias in surveys: asking only your friends, or wording a question to push an answer.
- Review reliability checks for a source: who collected it, how, when, for what purpose, and whether others agree.

## Guided Practice (6 minutes)

1. Measure one object three times and compare. Discuss why the results differ.
2. Design a fair survey question and decide who should be asked.
3. Compare the two prepared sources using the reliability checks.
4. Ask, "Which would you use, and what would you say about the other?"

## Independent Practice / Quick Check (4 minutes)

The student explains the most accurate way to collect data for a question you pose, such as which room in the house is warmest, and evaluates one source you name.

Look for:
- The method chosen fits the question and controls for error.
- Reliability is judged with specific checks.
- The student recognizes bias.

## Differentiation

**For students needing support**
- Compare measuring with estimating.
- Use three reliability checks: who, how, when.

**For students ready for more**
- Collect the same data two ways and compare accuracy.
- Rewrite a biased survey question to make it fair.

## Extensions

- Collect temperature data in several rooms over a day.
- Evaluate the source of a statistic heard in an advertisement.

## Notes for Parent/Instructor

- Accuracy is about method. Reliability is about the source. Both affect whether data can be trusted.
- Repeating a measurement is the simplest accuracy habit.
