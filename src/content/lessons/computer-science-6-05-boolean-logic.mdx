**Standards covered (2024 Virginia Computer Science SOL)**

- **6.AP.2** The student will plan and implement algorithms that include conditional control structures and collection of numeric data using a block-based or text-based tool.
  - e. Predict the results of logic expressions that use Boolean operators: and, or, and not; including expressions that use relational expressions as one or more operands.

## Prep

- Write expression cards, such as: age is at least 12 AND height is over 48; score is greater than 90 OR bonus is true; NOT raining; temperature is below 32 OR (windy AND temperature is below 45).

## Warm-up (3 minutes)

Say, "You may have dessert if you finish dinner and clear your plate." Then, "...if you finish dinner or clear your plate." Ask, "What changed?"

## Direct Instruction (5 minutes)

- Review relational expressions: comparisons such as less than, greater than, equal to, and not equal to. Each one is true or false.
- Define the Boolean operators. AND is true only when both sides are true. OR is true when at least one side is true. NOT reverses true and false.
- Build a truth table for AND and OR using two coins for true and false.
- Evaluate a combined expression step by step: work out each comparison first, then apply the operators, doing what is in parentheses first.

## Guided Practice (7 minutes)

1. The student completes the truth table for NOT and for A AND NOT B.
2. Give values for the variables on one card. The student predicts the result.
3. Change one value and predict again.
4. The student writes an expression for a real rule, such as who may ride a roller coaster.

## Independent Practice / Quick Check (4 minutes)

The student predicts the results of four expression cards for values you supply, showing the steps, and writes one expression that uses two operators.

Look for:
- Comparisons are evaluated before operators.
- AND, OR, and NOT are applied correctly.
- Parentheses are respected.

## Differentiation

**For students needing support**
- Work with AND and OR on simple true and false values before adding comparisons.
- Use the coins for every step.

**For students ready for more**
- Show that NOT (A AND B) gives the same results as (NOT A) OR (NOT B).
- Simplify a long expression without changing its results.

## Extensions

- Write household rules as Boolean expressions.
- Use advanced search with AND, OR, and NOT and predict the results.

## Notes for Parent/Instructor

- An operand is a value an operator works on. Here the operands are often comparisons.
- Students tend to read OR as "one or the other but not both." In logic it includes both.
