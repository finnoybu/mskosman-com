**Standards covered (2024 Virginia Computer Science SOL)**

- **6.IC.5** The student will describe the impacts of computing network architecture, including the role of the Internet in society.
  - a. Discuss ethical issues and laws related to accessibility, censorship, privacy, access, and safety while using the Internet.
  - b. Explain the role broadband connectivity has in social life, culture, and global economy.
- **6.NI.1** The student will outline the advantages and disadvantages of transmitting information over the Internet, including speed, reliability, cost, and security.
  - a. Explain the role of the Internet in social life, culture, and the economy.

## Prep

- For each issue card, prepare one current example and, where relevant, one law or policy, checked for accuracy.
- Find out what Internet service is available in your area and nearby rural areas.

## Warm-up (3 minutes)

Ask, "What would you be unable to do this week if your home had no Internet? What about your town?"

## Direct Instruction (6 minutes)

- Explain the Internet's role. Social life: staying connected and forming communities. Culture: sharing music, art, language, and ideas across the world. Economy: online business, remote work, and services.
- Define broadband: a high-speed Internet connection that is always on. Explain its role: without it, people have less access to school, work, health care, and services. This gap is called the digital divide.
- Introduce the five issues with your examples. Accessibility: can people with disabilities use it? Censorship: who decides what is blocked? Privacy: who can collect and use data? Access: who can get connected and afford it? Safety: how are people protected from harm?
- Explain that laws address some of these and differ between places.

## Guided Practice (6 minutes)

1. The student matches each example to an issue card.
2. For two issues, discuss competing views. What is the tension?
3. Discuss broadband locally: who has it and who does not?
4. Ask, "Should Internet access be treated like electricity or water? Why?"

## Independent Practice / Quick Check (4 minutes)

The student explains the Internet's role in social life, culture, and the economy, describes why broadband matters, and discusses one ethical issue with two viewpoints.

Look for:
- All three roles are addressed.
- Broadband is linked to opportunity.
- The ethical discussion presents more than one side.

## Differentiation

**For students needing support**
- Discuss three issues.
- Use local examples throughout.

**For students ready for more**
- Research the broadband situation in your county.
- Compare how two countries approach one of the issues.

## Extensions

- Interview someone who remembers life before home Internet.
- Follow a current news story about one of the five issues.

## Notes for Parent/Instructor

- Present viewpoints evenhandedly. Several of these issues are contested.
- This lesson combines two standards that both address the Internet's role in society.
