**Standards covered (2024 Virginia Computer Science SOL)**

- **3.DA.1** The student will gather, store, and organize data to evaluate trends and identify patterns using a computing device.
  - a. Formulate questions that require the collection or acquisition of data.
  - b. Gather, organize, sort, and store data.

## Prep

- Open a blank spreadsheet and check that you know how to type in cells, sort a column, and save the file.
- Think of data the student can gather quickly, such as the lengths of ten books in pages, or daily high temperatures for a week.

## Warm-up (3 minutes)

Ask two questions: "What is your favorite color?" and "What color car is most common on our street?" Ask, "Which one do we need to collect data to answer?"

## Direct Instruction (5 minutes)

- Explain: "A data question cannot be answered just by thinking. You have to collect or look up data."
- Model turning a wondering into a data question: "Are longer books always for older readers?"
- Show the spreadsheet: columns have headings, rows hold one item each, and each box is a cell.
- Enter two rows. Show how to sort a column from smallest to largest.
- Save the file with a clear name and show where it is stored.

## Guided Practice (8 minutes)

1. The student formulates their own data question.
2. Decide together what to record and type the column headings.
3. The student gathers the data and enters it, one row per item.
4. The student sorts by one column, then saves the file.

## Independent Practice / Quick Check (4 minutes)

The student writes one more data question about a different topic and tells you what columns it would need. Then the student closes the spreadsheet, finds it again, and opens it.

Look for:
- The question cannot be answered without data.
- Data is organized in labeled columns with one item per row.
- The student can sort and can retrieve the stored file.

## Differentiation

**For students needing support**
- Gather the data on paper first, then type it in together.
- Use two columns and six rows.

**For students ready for more**
- Sort by a second column and describe what changes.
- Add a column that is calculated from the others.

## Extensions

- Add to the spreadsheet over a week.
- Start a new spreadsheet for a collection, such as rocks, cards, or books read.

## Notes for Parent/Instructor

- This standard specifically says "using a computing device." Any spreadsheet program works.
- Keep the file. It is used in Lessons 14 and 15.
