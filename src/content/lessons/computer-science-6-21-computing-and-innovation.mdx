**Standards covered (2024 Virginia Computer Science SOL)**

- **6.IC.1** The student will assess the impact of computing technologies on local society.
  - a. Explain how computing impacts innovation and describe the development of new computing technologies in communication, entertainment, and business.
  - b. Discuss how computing technologies have influenced various industries and sectors locally.

## Prep

- List four or five industries or employers in your area, such as health care, farming, retail, manufacturing, tourism, or government, and note one way each uses computing.

## Warm-up (3 minutes)

Ask, "How did people watch a movie at home 40 years ago, 20 years ago, and today? What had to be invented for each step?"

## Direct Instruction (5 minutes)

- Define innovation: a new idea, method, or product that changes how things are done.
- Explain that computing enables innovation because faster processors, more storage, and networks make new things possible, and each innovation becomes a base for the next.
- Trace development in three areas. Communication: letters, phone calls, email, messaging, video calls. Entertainment: broadcast, discs, downloads, streaming, interactive games. Business: paper records, spreadsheets, online stores, digital payments.
- Introduce the local list and describe how one industry changed.

## Guided Practice (6 minutes)

1. The student builds the three-column timeline.
2. For each area, name what computing advance made the latest step possible.
3. Discuss two local industries: how was the work done before, and how now?
4. Ask, "Who gained from the change? Who lost?"

## Independent Practice / Quick Check (4 minutes)

The student explains how computing led to one innovation in each of the three areas and describes how computing has changed one local industry.

Look for:
- Innovations are linked to computing advances.
- All three areas are addressed.
- The local example is specific.

## Differentiation

**For students needing support**
- Build the timeline for one area.
- Discuss one local industry the student has seen firsthand.

**For students ready for more**
- Interview a local business owner about a technology change.
- Predict the next step in one timeline and what it would require.

## Extensions

- Visit a local workplace and note the computing technology in use.
- Add local examples to the timeline.

## Notes for Parent/Instructor

- Use real local examples. They are more convincing than national ones.
- Include losses as well as gains.
