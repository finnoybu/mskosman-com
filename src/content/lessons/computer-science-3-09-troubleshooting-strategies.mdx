**Standards covered (2024 Virginia Computer Science SOL)**

- **3.CSY.2** The student will use accurate terminology when troubleshooting problems when a computing system is not working as expected.
  - a. Identify common troubleshooting strategies used to address a variety of hardware and software problems.
  - b. Explain and apply troubleshooting strategies related to simple hardware and software problems.

## Prep

- Write six strategy cards: check power and cables; check the connection to the network; close and reopen the program; restart the device; check settings such as volume or brightness; look up the error message or ask for help.
- Set up one safe problem, such as the device being disconnected from Wi-Fi or the brightness turned all the way down.

## Warm-up (3 minutes)

Describe a problem vaguely: "The thing is not doing the thing." Ask what is unclear. Then restate it accurately.

## Direct Instruction (5 minutes)

- Review accurate terms: hardware, software, input, output, processor, network, app, restart, settings.
- Introduce the strategy cards one at a time and say when each one helps.
- Sort the cards together into strategies mainly for hardware problems and strategies mainly for software problems. Some fit both.
- Explain the reason behind one strategy: restarting clears out programs that are stuck.

## Guided Practice (6 minutes)

1. Present the staged problem. The student describes it using accurate terms.
2. The student chooses a strategy card, explains why, and applies it.
3. If it does not work, choose the next card.
4. The student records in the log: the problem, the strategy, and the result.

## Independent Practice / Quick Check (5 minutes)

Describe two problems aloud. For each, the student names the strategy to try first, explains why, and says how they would describe the problem to someone helping over the phone.

Look for:
- The student identifies several strategies and when each applies.
- Descriptions use accurate terms.
- The student explains the reasoning for a strategy, not just the action.

## Differentiation

**For students needing support**
- Use three strategy cards.
- Give the sentence frame: "The ___ is not ___, so I will try ___."

**For students ready for more**
- Decide the best order for all six strategies and justify it.
- Write a short help guide for a problem your family has had.

## Extensions

- Keep the log going for real problems through the year.
- Teach a family member the six strategies.

## Notes for Parent/Instructor

- The emphasis this year is on accurate terminology. Gently ask for the precise word when the student says "thing" or "it."
- Agree in advance which strategies need your permission, such as restarting or changing settings.
