**Standards covered (2024 Virginia Computer Science SOL)**

- **7.IC.4** The student will identify and apply strategies to prevent personal and public works from being pirated and plagiarized.
  - a. Discuss and describe intellectual property protections.
  - b. Research and list safeguards used to prevent intellectual property infringement.

## Prep

- Bookmark an official or educational page explaining copyright, patents, trademarks, and trade secrets in plain language.
- Choose a piece of the student's writing, art, or code.

## Warm-up (3 minutes)

Ask, "You post your artwork online and a week later someone is selling shirts with it. What could you do?"

## Direct Instruction (5 minutes)

- Describe the four protections. Copyright: creative works such as writing, music, art, and code. Patent: inventions and processes. Trademark: names, logos, and symbols that identify a source. Trade secret: valuable information kept confidential.
- Define infringement: using protected work without permission. Piracy is unauthorized copying or distribution. Plagiarism is presenting another's work as your own.
- Introduce safeguards. Legal: copyright notices, registration, and licenses. Technical: watermarks, lower-resolution previews, access controls, and plagiarism detection. Practical: keeping dated drafts and originals as proof, and citing sources properly.

## Guided Practice (7 minutes)

1. The student matches four examples to the right protection.
2. Read the source and list safeguards it mentions.
3. Sort the safeguards into legal, technical, and practical.
4. Apply two to the student's own work.

## Independent Practice / Quick Check (4 minutes)

The student describes two intellectual property protections, lists five safeguards against infringement, and explains which they would use for their own work and why.

Look for:
- Protections are matched to the right kinds of work.
- Safeguards are specific.
- The student applies safeguards to real work.

## Differentiation

**For students needing support**
- Focus on copyright and trademark.
- List three safeguards.

**For students ready for more**
- Compare how an artist, a musician, and a software company protect their work.
- Explain how plagiarism detection works in general terms.

## Extensions

- Add a notice and license to a personal project.
- Keep dated drafts of the next big assignment.

## Notes for Parent/Instructor

- Both sides matter: protecting your own work and respecting others'.
- Laws differ by country. Use a reliable source.
