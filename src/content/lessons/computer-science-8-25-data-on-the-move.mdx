**Standards covered (2024 Virginia Computer Science SOL)**

- **8.NI.1** The student will model and describe the role of computing devices in transmitting data in and on computing networks and the Internet.
  - c. Demonstrate how data is transmitted over networks and the Internet.
  - d. Analyze factors that strengthen or weaken network connectivity.

## Prep

- Decide on one request to demonstrate, such as a client loading a web page from a server on the Internet.

## Warm-up (3 minutes)

Ask, "Why is Wi-Fi strong in one room and weak in another? Why is it slow at 7 in the evening?"

## Direct Instruction (4 minutes)

- Review the transmission sequence: the client makes a request; data is split into packets with addresses; the switch passes them to the router; routers forward them across networks to the server; the server responds; packets return and are reassembled.
- Explain what each device contributes at each step.
- Name factors that strengthen connectivity: wired connections, a strong signal, enough bandwidth, modern equipment, redundant paths, and good placement of access points.
- Name factors that weaken it: distance and obstacles, interference from other devices and networks, congestion from many users, outdated or faulty equipment, and a single point of failure.

## Guided Practice (9 minutes)

1. The student moves packet tokens across the topology for the request and the response, narrating each step.
2. Introduce a failed connection. The student shows how data is rerouted, or explains why it cannot be.
3. Optional: measure signal strength in three places and explain the differences.
4. Complete the factors chart: strengthens or weakens, and why.

## Independent Practice / Quick Check (4 minutes)

The student demonstrates a second transmission of their choice from start to finish and analyzes the network design for two factors that could weaken it and how to strengthen it.

Look for:
- The demonstration follows the correct sequence through each device.
- Each device's role is explained.
- Factors are analyzed with causes and remedies.

## Differentiation

**For students needing support**
- Demonstrate a transmission within the local network only.
- Chart four factors.

**For students ready for more**
- Demonstrate what changes when the data is encrypted.
- Propose and justify upgrades to the network within a budget.

## Extensions

- Diagnose a weak spot in your home network and test a fix with an adult.
- Explain the demonstration to a younger student.

## Notes for Parent/Instructor

- The standard's verbs are "demonstrate" and "analyze." Moving tokens across the topology covers the first, and the chart covers the second.
- This lesson closes the networks strand for the middle grades.
