**Standards covered (2024 Virginia Computer Science SOL)**

- **2.IC.3** The student will explain how computing technologies have an impact on the workforce.
  - a. Explain how computing technology is used in various careers.
  - b. Identify skills needed for careers that use computing technologies.
  - c. Discuss how computing technologies have changed the workplace.

## Prep

- Make the career cards.
- For each career, jot down one technology and one way the job was done before that technology.
- If possible, arrange a five-minute conversation with a working adult.

## Warm-up (3 minutes)

Ask, "What job would you like to try someday? Do you think that job uses computers?"

## Direct Instruction (5 minutes)

- Explain: "Almost every career uses computing technology in some way."
- Model one row: a mechanic plugs a computer into a car to find out what is wrong.
- Introduce skills: "A skill is something you learn to do well." Skills for careers that use technology include typing, solving problems step by step, paying attention to detail, communicating clearly, and learning new tools.
- Describe change in the workplace: "Before, a cashier typed in every price. Now a scanner reads it. Some people now work from home using video calls."
- Note that technology changes some jobs, ends some, and creates new ones.

## Guided Practice (7 minutes)

1. The student fills in a row for three more careers, with your prompts.
2. For each, ask, "What skill would that worker need to use the technology well?"
3. Ask how each job might have been done before the technology existed.
4. Hold the conversation with the working adult, or tell the student about a job you have had.

## Independent Practice / Quick Check (4 minutes)

The student chooses one career, explains how it uses computing technology, names two skills it needs, and describes one way technology changed that kind of work.

Look for:
- The explanation of technology use fits the career.
- The skills named are real and relevant.
- The student describes a change over time.

## Differentiation

**For students needing support**
- Use careers of people the student knows.
- Act out the job before and after the technology.

**For students ready for more**
- Find a job that did not exist when you were a child.
- Ask which skills on the list the student is already building.

## Extensions

- Interview two adults about technology at work and compare their answers.
- Draw a before-and-after picture of one workplace.

## Notes for Parent/Instructor

- Grade 1 listed technologies used in careers. Grade 2 adds skills and change over time.
- Real stories from adults the student knows are the best material for this lesson.
