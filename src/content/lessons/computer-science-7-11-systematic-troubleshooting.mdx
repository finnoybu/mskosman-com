**Standards covered (2024 Virginia Computer Science SOL)**

- **7.CSY.2** The student will apply computational thinking to troubleshoot and document hardware and software-related problems.
  - a. Apply systematic processes to resolve hardware, software, and connectivity-related problems.
  - b. Compile and record successful methods used to resolve problems for common hardware, software, and connectivity-related problems.

## Prep

- Stage a safe problem with more than one possible cause, such as no sound: muted, wrong output selected, or unplugged speakers.
- Create a knowledge base template: Problem, Symptoms, Cause, Steps that worked, Date.

## Warm-up (3 minutes)

Ask, "Have you ever fixed something and later forgotten how? What would have saved you?"

## Direct Instruction (4 minutes)

- Frame troubleshooting as computational thinking: decompose the system into parts, look for patterns from past problems, abstract away what is irrelevant, and follow an algorithm.
- Teach a systematic process: define the problem precisely; list possible causes; order them by likelihood and ease of checking; test one at a time; confirm the fix; document it.
- Explain isolating a cause: change one thing at a time, and swap in a known good part when possible.
- Introduce the knowledge base: a searchable record of problems and solutions.

## Guided Practice (8 minutes)

1. Present the staged problem. The student defines it and lists possible causes.
2. Order the causes and test them one at a time.
3. Confirm the fix by testing again.
4. Write the first knowledge base entry.

## Independent Practice / Quick Check (4 minutes)

Describe a second problem in words. The student applies the process on paper and adds entries for two problems the family has solved before.

Look for:
- Causes are listed and tested one at a time.
- The fix is confirmed.
- Entries are clear enough for someone else to follow.

## Differentiation

**For students needing support**
- Use a problem with two possible causes.
- Fill in the first entry together.

**For students ready for more**
- Add a decision tree for a common problem to the knowledge base.
- Organize entries with tags so they can be searched.

## Extensions

- Maintain the knowledge base for the family through the year.
- Contribute a solution to someone who has the same problem.

## Notes for Parent/Instructor

- Documentation is the new element at this grade.
- Testing one cause at a time is slower in the moment and faster overall.
