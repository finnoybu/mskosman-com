**Standards covered (2024 Virginia Computer Science SOL)**

- **3.IC.2** The student will discuss and describe strategies to manage screen time.
  - a. Define and describe screen time.
  - b. Explain the importance of responsible screen time management.
  - c. Discuss how screen time choices affect one’s personal health and interactions with others.

## Prep

- If possible, have the student keep the log for one day before the lesson.

## Warm-up (3 minutes)

Ask the student to estimate how many minutes they spent on screens yesterday. Compare with the log if you have one.

## Direct Instruction (4 minutes)

- Define: "Screen time is the time spent using a device with a screen, such as a tablet, computer, phone, television, or game console."
- Explain management: deciding ahead of time how much, when, and for what purpose.
- Explain why it matters: too much or badly timed screen time can crowd out sleep, movement, learning, and time with people.
- Discuss effects. On health: tired eyes, poor sleep, less activity. On interactions: less talking and listening, or feeling ignored when someone is on a device. Also name positives: learning and staying in touch.

## Guided Practice (5 minutes)

1. Look at the log. The student totals the minutes and labels each use as learning, entertainment, or communication.
2. Ask, "Did any screen time get in the way of something else?"
3. Together, choose two management strategies, such as a timer, a screen-free place, or a set time of day.
4. Role-play: you look at a phone while the student tries to tell you something. Ask how it felt.

## Independent Practice / Quick Check (3 minutes)

The student defines screen time, explains one reason to manage it, gives one effect on health and one on relationships, and writes a simple plan for tomorrow.

Look for:
- The definition covers different kinds of screens.
- The reasons are about real effects.
- The plan includes a specific strategy.

## Differentiation

**For students needing support**
- Log half a day.
- Choose one strategy and practice it for a week.

**For students ready for more**
- Graph a week of screen time by purpose using Lesson 15.
- Compare two days and explain the difference.

## Extensions

- Hold a family meeting to agree on one shared screen habit.
- Try the plan for a week and review how it went.

## Notes for Parent/Instructor

- The limits are yours to set. The student's job here is understanding and planning.
- Being honest about your own screen habits makes this lesson more convincing.
