**Standards covered (2024 Virginia Computer Science SOL)**

- **1.CYB.1** The student will demonstrate safe and responsible use of computing technologies.
  - b. Demonstrate safe and responsible behaviors when using computing technologies and online communication.
  - c. Discuss the process for reporting inappropriate technology use at school or home.

## Prep

- Arrange for a trusted family member to receive a short message during the lesson.
- Decide the reporting steps for your home and who the trusted grown-ups are.

## Warm-up (3 minutes)

Ask, "How do you show kindness when you talk to someone face to face?" Then ask, "The person reading a message cannot see your face or hear your voice. What might be different?"

## Direct Instruction (5 minutes)

- Teach three habits for online communication:
  - **Be safe**: only communicate with people a trusted grown-up has approved. Do not share private information.
  - **Be kind**: use words you would say to the person's face.
  - **Ask first**: check with a grown-up before sending, clicking, or opening.
- Model writing a short message to the family member. Think aloud: "Is this kind? Is it going to someone we know? Did I share anything private?"
- Teach reporting: "Inappropriate use means someone breaks the rules, is unkind, or something on the screen feels wrong or scary."
- Teach the reporting steps:
  1. Stop what you are doing.
  2. Leave the screen as it is. Do not delete anything.
  3. Tell a trusted grown-up right away.
- Say, "Reporting is how we get help. You will not be in trouble for telling."

## Guided Practice (6 minutes)

1. The student composes a short, kind message to the family member. You type or help as needed.
2. Before sending, the student checks the three habits aloud. Then send it together.
3. Role-play: "You are playing a game and a message pops up from someone you do not know." The student shows the reporting steps.
4. Role-play: "A friend shows you something unkind on their tablet." The student says what they would do.

## Independent Practice / Quick Check (4 minutes)

Ask the student to show you, on the device, how they get ready to use it responsibly and what they check before sending a message. Then ask, "What are the steps if you see something that is not okay? Who would you tell at home? Who would you tell somewhere else?"

Look for:
- The student demonstrates the safe habits while using the device.
- The student states the reporting steps in order.
- The student names trusted grown-ups for more than one setting.

## Differentiation

**For students needing support**
- Send a voice message or a drawing in place of typed words.
- Post the three reporting steps as pictures.
- Rehearse one role-play several times.

**For students ready for more**
- Compare two messages and decide which is kinder and why.
- Discuss what to do when a friend asks you to keep something on a screen secret.

## Extensions

- Draw a reporting poster with the three steps.
- Write a thank-you message to a relative with a grown-up's help.

## Notes for Parent/Instructor

- You operate or closely supervise the device for all online communication in this lesson.
- The standard says "at school or home." Help the student name who to tell in each place they use technology.
- Leaving the screen as it is helps you see what happened. Reassure the student that telling you is always right.
