**Standards covered (2024 Virginia Computer Science SOL)**

- **6.NI.1** The student will outline the advantages and disadvantages of transmitting information over the Internet, including speed, reliability, cost, and security.
  - b. Explain data transfer and the impact of connectivity speed when data is going from one device to another.
  - c. Compare the speed and reliability of various data transmission media.
  - d. Describe the advantages and disadvantages of transporting information over the Internet.

## Prep

- Prepare brief, accurate notes comparing the five media on speed, reliability, and typical use.
- Find out what kind of connection your home uses.

## Warm-up (3 minutes)

Ask, "Why does a large file take longer to send than a short message, even on the same connection?"

## Direct Instruction (5 minutes)

- Explain data transfer: data is broken into packets that travel from one device to another through the network.
- Define speed terms. Bandwidth: how much data can move per second, measured in megabits per second. Latency: the delay before data arrives. A faster connection means quicker downloads, smoother video, and less lag.
- Compare the media using your notes. In general, fiber carries data as light and is fastest and very reliable; copper cable is reliable but slower; Wi-Fi is convenient but weakens with distance and obstacles; cellular reaches wide areas; satellite reaches remote places but usually with more delay.
- Chart the Internet's advantages and disadvantages for sending information across four factors: speed, reliability, cost, and security.

## Guided Practice (7 minutes)

1. The student completes the media comparison table.
2. Match three situations to the best medium: a rural home, a hospital, a moving car.
3. Optional: run a speed test and interpret the results.
4. Complete the advantages and disadvantages chart together.

## Independent Practice / Quick Check (4 minutes)

The student explains how connection speed affects data transfer, compares two transmission media, and gives one advantage and one disadvantage of the Internet for each of the four factors.

Look for:
- Speed is explained in terms of data per second and delay.
- The media comparison is accurate.
- All four factors are addressed.

## Differentiation

**For students needing support**
- Compare three media.
- Use the road comparison: lanes for bandwidth and travel time for latency.

**For students ready for more**
- Measure speed at different times of day and explain the variation.
- Compare sending a large file over the Internet with carrying it on a drive.

## Extensions

- Find out how your Internet reaches your home.
- Compare service options available in your area.

## Notes for Parent/Instructor

- Details of each medium vary. Teach the general comparison and check specifics.
- Security is one of the four factors: data sent over the Internet can be intercepted unless it is protected.
