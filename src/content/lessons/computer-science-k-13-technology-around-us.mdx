**Standards covered (2024 Virginia Computer Science SOL)**

- **K.IC.1** The student will identify and discuss how computing technologies impact people’s lives.
  - a. List computing technologies found in the classroom, home, and the community.
  - b. Identify how computing technologies are used in daily tasks.
  - c. Discuss healthy habits for using computing technologies.

## Prep

- Divide a large sheet of paper into three sections and add a quick drawing to each: a learning space, a home, and a community place.
- Think of two computing technologies the student has seen out in the community.

## Warm-up (3 minutes)

Ask, "What technology have you used or seen today?" If the student is unsure, prompt with, "Did anything light up, beep, or show a picture?"

## Direct Instruction (5 minutes)

- Explain: "Computing technologies are tools that use a tiny computer inside to help people do things."
- Point to the three sections of the paper. Name one or two examples for each and draw a quick sketch:
  - Learning space: tablet, laptop.
  - Home: phone, television, a speaker you can talk to.
  - Community: the scanner at the store checkout, the screen at the library, a traffic light.
- For each, say what daily task it helps with: "The scanner helps the cashier add up what we buy."
- Shift to healthy habits: "Technology is helpful, and our bodies and brains also need other things."
- Teach four habits: take breaks and move your body; rest your eyes by looking far away; screens off at meals and before sleep; balance screen time with play, books, and time with people.

## Guided Practice (5 minutes)

1. Walk through the home or learning space together. The student points out computing technologies, and you add each to the correct section of the paper.
2. For each one, ask, "What does it help someone do?"
3. Think about the community: "What technology did we see the last time we went to the store or the doctor?"
4. Practice a habit right now: set the timer for one minute and do a stretch and look-out-the-window break.

## Independent Practice / Quick Check (3 minutes)

Ask the student to:

1. Name one computing technology in each of the three places.
2. Choose two and tell what daily task each helps with.
3. Tell you two healthy habits for using technology.

Look for:
- At least one example for the learning space, home, and community.
- A use that matches each technology named.
- At least two healthy habits stated in the student's own words.

## Differentiation

**For students needing support**
- Focus on home and learning space first, and add community on your next outing.
- Use photos of the actual items in your home.
- Practice one healthy habit until it is routine before adding another.

**For students ready for more**
- Ask how a daily task was done before the technology existed.
- The student explains why each healthy habit helps the body.

## Extensions

- Play "technology I spy" on the next trip to a store or library.
- Make a healthy habits chart with a picture for each habit and check them off for a week.

## Notes for Parent/Instructor

- The standard names "the classroom, home, and the community." For homeschool and tutoring, the learning space stands in for the classroom.
- Set your family's own limits on screen time. This lesson teaches the habits, and the specific amounts are your decision.
- Keep the discussion balanced: technology helps people, and healthy habits help people use it well.
