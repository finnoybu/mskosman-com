**Standards covered (2024 Virginia Computer Science SOL)**

- **4.DA.1** The student will identify the appropriate type of data needed to solve a problem or answer a question.
  - d. Collect, store, clean, and organize data for analysis and to prepare visualizations.
- **4.DA.2** The student will create and evaluate data representations to make predictions and conclusions.
  - a. Formulate questions that require the collection or acquisition of data.
  - b. Collect data to create charts and graphs.

## Prep

- Choose data that can be gathered in a few minutes or beforehand, such as family members' daily steps, books read per month, or a tally of car colors.
- Review how to sort and insert a chart.

## Warm-up (2 minutes)

Show a messy list with entries such as "blue", "Blue", "bleu", and a blank. Ask, "How many blue cars is that? What makes it hard to count?"

## Direct Instruction (4 minutes)

- Review a data question: one that needs data to answer.
- Define cleaning: fixing or removing errors so the data can be trusted. Make spelling and capital letters consistent, remove duplicates, fix or flag impossible values, and deal with blanks.
- Organize: one item per row, a heading on each column, and one kind of data in each column.
- Review making a chart and choosing a type that fits.

## Guided Practice (9 minutes)

1. The student writes the data question.
2. The student collects the data and enters it into the spreadsheet.
3. Together, look for problems and clean them.
4. The student sorts the data, creates a chart, and adds a title and labels.

## Independent Practice / Quick Check (5 minutes)

The student saves the file, then explains what was cleaned and why, and why the chart type fits the question.

Look for:
- The question requires data.
- Cleaning steps are specific and justified.
- The chart matches the cleaned data and is labeled.

## Differentiation

**For students needing support**
- Enter the data together and let the student do the cleaning.
- Use ten rows or fewer.

**For students ready for more**
- Keep an untouched copy of the original data and explain why that is good practice.
- Make two chart types and compare.

## Extensions

- Add to the dataset for a week and update the chart.
- Clean a contact list or a collection list at home.

## Notes for Parent/Instructor

- Cleaning never means changing data to get the answer you want. Say so directly.
- Keep the file for Lesson 19.
